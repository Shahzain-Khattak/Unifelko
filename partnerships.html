Partnerships

Grow with UNIFELKO

We connect customers with independent service providers. If you are one of those providers — or a business that needs a local partner — we would like to hear from you.

Who we work with

Three ways to partner with us

UNIFELKO sits between demand and delivery. That means partners on both sides of the connection — the people who provide services, and the businesses that need reach.

Service providers

Workshops, contractors, agents, transporters, technicians and specialist firms who deliver real work. If customers with matching requirements come your way, we would like you to be the one we call.

Businesses

Companies entering a new market, or without a local presence, who need a point of contact on the ground — to handle enquiries, coordinate suppliers and represent their interests locally.

Independent professionals

Skilled individuals who take on work directly. Being part of our network means your name is among the first considered when a suitable requirement arrives.

The benefit

What a partnership offers

We are not a directory listing and we do not sell leads. A UNIFELKO partnership is an ongoing working relationship where both sides know what to expect.

  • Requirements that already match — we only introduce work that fits what you actually do.
  • A clearer brief — by the time we contact you, the requirement has been understood and structured.
  • One point of coordination — you deal with UNIFELKO rather than juggling many separate contacts.
  • Visibility across categories — a presence in automotive, property, business and on-demand enquiries.
  • Room to grow — as the relationship develops, so does the range of work routed your way.

What we ask of partners

  • Be straightforward about what you can and cannot deliver.
  • Respond to introductions in a reasonable time.
  • Give honest quotes and clear timelines directly to the customer.
  • Keep us informed so we can coordinate properly.
  • Treat every referred customer the same way you would your own.

In fairness to everyone: we cannot promise a fixed volume of work, exclusive arrangements or guaranteed outcomes. Partnerships are discussed and agreed case by case, and they work best when the fit is genuinely right.

Need someone local on the ground?
