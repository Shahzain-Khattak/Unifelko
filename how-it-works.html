How it works

Five simple steps, start to finish

No complicated onboarding, no forms in triplicate. You make contact, we do the understanding and the coordinating — and the provider does the work.

The process

From first message to finished service

Every enquiry follows the same five steps, whatever the category or the size of the requirement.

1

A customer contacts UNIFELKO

Reach us whichever way suits you — phone, WhatsApp, email or the enquiry form on this site. There is no registration process and no obligation. A short message describing what you need is enough to begin.

2

We understand your requirements

We ask the questions that matter: what exactly you need, where, by when, and any constraints that apply. This step is where most of our value sits — a requirement understood properly is a requirement matched properly.

3

We connect you with a suitable service provider

Based on the requirement, we introduce an independent provider whose capabilities fit it. We share the relevant details so neither side has to start from zero, and you decide whether to proceed.

4

The service provider handles the required service

From here, the provider works with you directly — quoting, scheduling and delivering. Any commercial terms, pricing and timelines are agreed between you and them.

5

We coordinate where needed

If something needs chasing, clarifying or re-aligning, we stay involved so the connection does not go cold. Our role continues for as long as our coordination is useful to both sides.

Getting started

What helps us help you

You do not need a formal brief. But a few basics let us identify the right provider much faster.

  • What — the outcome you want, in your own words.
  • Where — the city, area or market involved.
  • When — your timeframe, even if it is approximate.
  • Constraints — budget range, specifications or deadlines.
  • How to reach you — the best contact method and a good time.

Who does what

  • UNIFELKO — understands the requirement, makes the connection and coordinates.
  • The provider — quotes, schedules and delivers the actual service.
  • You — approve the connection, agree the terms and decide when to proceed.

UNIFELKO does not employ the providers we connect you with, and we do not set their prices. Agreements are made directly between you and the provider.

What to expect

After you get in touch

A reply, then a conversation

We respond to your enquiry and follow up with a few questions so the requirement is clear before anyone else is contacted.

An introduction, not a commitment

If we have a suitable provider, we introduce you. If we do not, we say so rather than pass you to someone who does not fit.

Ongoing coordination

Once the connection is live, we remain available so that anything needing a third pair of hands can be sorted out quickly.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to pay UNIFELKO anything to make an enquiry?
Contacting us and discussing your requirement carries no charge. If any commercial arrangement applies to your case, that is discussed with you and agreed before any work begins — never after.
Is UNIFELKO the company that will do the work?
No. UNIFELKO is a third-party service facilitator. We understand your requirement and connect you with an independent provider who handles the service. We are not a car dealership, property agency or logistics company, and we do not employ the providers we introduce.
Who sets the price for the service?
The provider does. Quotations, pricing, scope and timelines are agreed directly between you and the provider handling the work. UNIFELKO does not set those terms or make promises on a provider's behalf.
What if you cannot find a suitable provider?
We tell you. Provider availability depends on your location, your requirement and timing, so we will be straightforward about what we can and cannot arrange rather than introduce you to someone unsuitable.
Which areas do you cover?
Our reach depends on the category and the provider network available for that requirement. Share your location when you enquire and we will confirm what is possible in your case.
Can businesses work with UNIFELKO too?
Yes. We work with companies that need a local point of contact, supplier and partner connections, or coordination in a market they are entering. See the Partnerships page for details.
