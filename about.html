About UNIFELKO

One partner for many different needs

UNIFELKO Private Limited exists to make one thing easier: reaching the right service provider without searching, guessing or starting over with every new requirement.

Introduction

Who we are

UNIFELKO Private Limited is an international business and service facilitation company. We connect buyers, sellers and dealers with reliable third-party services and practical, well-matched solutions.

Most people are not short of options — they are short of time and certainty. Finding a dependable provider, explaining the requirement repeatedly, and chasing progress takes energy away from the thing you actually wanted done.

UNIFELKO sits in the middle of that gap. You come to us with the requirement; we understand it, connect you with an independent provider suited to it, and coordinate where our involvement helps.

What drives us

Vision, mission and approach

Our vision

To be the first call people make when they need something arranged — because they know one reliable partner can reach the right provider across many different needs.

Our mission

To make connecting with a suitable service provider simple, honest and low-effort — by understanding requirements properly and coordinating the connection with care.

Our values

Clarity before connection, honesty about what we can do, respect for your time, and consistency — the same standard of attention for every enquiry, whatever its size.

Business approach

How we work with customers and businesses

Our approach is deliberately simple. It is the same whether you are an individual with one requirement or a business looking for an ongoing partner.

1. Listen before recommending

We start by understanding the requirement in detail — what, where, when and why. Only when that is clear do we think about which kind of provider fits.

2. Stay honest about our role

We facilitate the connection — we are not the provider. We are straightforward about what we can arrange, and equally clear when something is outside what we handle.

3. Leave the choice with you

We introduce a suitable provider; you decide whether to proceed. Terms, pricing and delivery are agreed directly between you and that provider.

4. Keep communicating

Once the connection is made, we coordinate where needed so neither side is left wondering what happens next.

Our position

What UNIFELKO is — and what we are not

Being clear about this from the start saves everyone time later.

What we are

  • A third-party service facilitation company operating across several industries.
  • A single point of contact for requirements you would rather not research yourself.
  • A coordinator who stays involved after the introduction is made.
  • A local representation option for businesses entering a new market.

What we are not

  • Not a car dealership, property agency or logistics company — we do not sell the service ourselves.
  • Not the employer of the providers we connect you with; they run their own businesses.
  • Not a party to your agreement with the provider — that is between the two of you.
  • Not in the business of making promises on a provider's behalf — we leave claims to those doing the work.

In practice: you describe the need, we connect you with a suitable independent provider, and the provider handles the service directly with you. UNIFELKO coordinates where our involvement is useful — and nothing is arranged behind your back.
