Who we are

A facilitator — not a single-industry company

UNIFELKO Private Limited is an international business and service facilitation company. We are not a car dealership, a property agency or a logistics firm. Our role sits between you and the people who actually deliver the service.

Tell us what you are trying to achieve. We listen, clarify the requirement, and connect you with an independent service provider who fits it. From there, we coordinate where our involvement is useful.

  • You describe the need — in your own words, without having to research providers first.
  • We identify the right kind of provider for that specific requirement.
  • You deal with a suitable provider, with UNIFELKO keeping the connection on track.
