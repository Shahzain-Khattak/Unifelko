Please note

How our service works in practice

Availability varies

Which providers we can connect you with depends on your location, your requirement and provider availability at the time. We will tell you where we stand.

Terms are agreed directly

Pricing, scope and timelines are agreed between you and the provider handling the service. We do not set or promise those on their behalf.

You stay in control

An introduction is an introduction. You decide whether to proceed, and you deal with the provider directly from that point on.
